Lessons

Patterns that killed candidates, and the check that avoids each.

#PatternEvidenceCheck before proposing
L1Domain changes can hide identical machinery.Synthetic R1/SIM-S1 and R5/SIM-S5 close two repackaged mechanisms.State the guarantee remaining after the strongest baseline, with a distinguishing trace.
L2Partition claims need an information premise.Synthetic R3/SIM-S3 defeats instantaneous revocation without a new local observation.Write two indistinguishable local histories before claiming a remote event is known.
L3Explanation polish is not yet an explanation contribution.Synthetic R6 is 90/87; SIM-S6 already previews operations. R9 relabels SIM-S8.Specify the user's decision and a different observable guarantee, not renamed controls.
L4Component novelty cannot hide a familiar overall idea.Synthetic R7: research novelty 95, idea novelty 92, so novelty is 92.Assess both dimensions and take their minimum; test the dependency-summary premise.

What has worked: Within the synthetic scenario, separating merge correctness, permission authority, recovery cost, and user agency exposed genuinely different next questions.
