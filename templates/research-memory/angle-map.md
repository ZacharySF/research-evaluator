## Status rules

- **closed**: the stated mechanism is ruled out by a specific source or argument; keep the closure source and a falsifiable reopen condition. Closing one claim does not close a whole field.
- **partial**: searched, with a concrete residual contribution, but below the configured bar or unresolved; name the leftover and limiting evidence.
- **active**: currently assigned/in progress, including work paused for a recorded prerequisite; say what check will complete it. It is not a success label.
- **open**: not yet searched. Explain why it is worth exploring; do not imply that a gap is established.

For every investigated angle, cite receipts and real source locations, version/date, what was actually read, relevance, and access/quality limits. Separate primary-source findings, secondary leads, inference, and hypotheses. Lack of search hits is not closure evidence or proof of novelty. Record actual reviewer mode. For blocked reviews preserve null scores and the exact missing prerequisite; do not guess numbers.
